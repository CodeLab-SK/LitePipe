LitePipe is a fast, lightweight, and feature-rich webview YouTube & YouTube Music client for Android.
It offers a completely ad-free experience with powerful extra features that the official YouTube app doesn’t provide.

## Key Features

- Ad-free YouTube and YouTube Music experience
- SponsorBlock – automatically skips sponsors, intros, outros, and self-promotions
- Return YouTube Dislike – shows dislike counts again
- Advanced custom player with subtitles, multiple audio tracks, and gesture controls
- Background playback and Picture-in-Picture (PiP) support
- Playback queue – add videos to a queue and keep watching
- Live chat support for streams
- Powerful built-in downloader – download videos, audio, shorts, captions, playlists, and thumbnails
- Local video player – play downloaded or local device videos with the same advanced player
- Incognito mode – watch without saving history
- Very lightweight – full APK size is only around 6 MB

# Other useful features

- Open YouTube links directly or download them from the share menu
- Can be set as the default app for YouTube links
- Supports signing in with your Google account
- Works alongside the official YouTube app
